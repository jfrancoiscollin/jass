and 1504 action-source pools.  Byte-identical CURRICULUM plays both sides at

middle 40% of profiles, so a smaller two-times extension would be expected to

3. applies the already frozen `(52, 154] cp` maximum depth-score-spread gate;
4. enforces canonical-state uniqueness and at most two states per game;
5. builds a phase/material/WDL/branching matched lattice; and
6. projects the later exact-label cost from the certified 1508 timing only.

The screen passes only with at least 1,800 eligible states, 900 games, 900
openings, total raw matching capacity 900, capacity 360 in each pool, and a

capacity headroom is deliberate because teacher disagreement remains unknown.

promotion is touched by the availability job.  A PASS authorizes one later
exact-label job.  That job must preserve the frozen candidate order, accept
the first 300 valid disagreement/agreement pairs, fit only the immutable 1508
